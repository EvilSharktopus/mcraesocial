Marathon Race Week

Nutrition Plan
Shopping List
May 10–16 — build & adapt
Main goal: start nudging carbs up so your gut is used to higher loads by race week. No restrictions yet — fibre, fat, and protein all stay normal.
Sat May 10rest — recover from May 9
~350g carbs
  • Big day yesterday (OTF + 90 min run) — prioritise recovery eating today
  • Focus on protein + carbs; don't undereat just because it's a rest day
  • Hydrate well — replenish what you lost Saturday
Sun May 11Strength PM
~350–400g carbs
  • Carbs before and after strength session as normal
  • Dinner is a good place to add an extra serving of rice, pasta, or bread
Mon May 12Intervals AM · Physio PM
~400–450g carbs
  • Intervals are your hardest session of this block — fuel it properly
  • Carb-rich breakfast before (oats, banana, toast); recover with carbs + protein after
  • Physio in the PM — hydrate extra before and after soft tissue work
Tue May 13Rest · Vietnamese dinner
~400–450g carbs
  • Vermicelli bowl is ideal — rice noodles are easy carbs, low fibre, low fat
  • Add spring rolls for extra carbs if appetite allows
  • Skip or minimise heavy broths with a lot of fat; fresh herbs and veg are fine at this stage
Wed May 14Recovery run AM · Strength PM
~400–450g carbs
  • Double session day — fuel both; don't go into strength training under-eaten
  • Keep carbs spread across morning, lunch, and dinner
Thu May 15rest
~400g carbs
  • Rest day — hold carbs steady, don't drop them
  • Stored glycogen from today carries into Friday's run
Fri May 161 hour run
~450g carbs
  • Last meaningful training run — treat it like a mini race-day dress rehearsal for fueling
  • Carb-rich breakfast before; good recovery meal after
  • Tonight's dinner is the bridge into race week — make it a proper carb meal (pasta, rice)
Sat May 17rest
~450g carbs
  • Rest day, but carbs stay elevated — you're pre-loading now
  • Light on fibre and very fatty foods tonight; gut prep starts here
May 18–21 — carb load
Target 500–600g carbs/day. This is a meaningful jump — you'll feel fuller than usual. That's glycogen + water binding to it. Normal and good.
Sun May 18Strength PM
~500–550g carbs
  • Last strength session — lighter load than usual, just maintain
  • Big carb dinner tonight: rice, pasta, or potatoes; keep fat moderate
  • Start avoiding high-fibre foods (bran, legumes, raw cruciferous veg) at dinner
Mon May 1930 min jog AM · Physio PM
~550g carbs
  • Jog is just to keep legs loose — not a training stimulus
  • Breakfast, lunch, and dinner all carb-heavy; protein stays consistent
  • Physio — hydrate well; they may work on legs pre-race
  • Snacks: bananas, white bread, rice cakes, pretzels — low fibre options
Tue May 20Massage PM · Vietnamese dinnergut-friendly
~550–600g carbs
  • Vermicelli bowl again — perfect timing, rice noodles are low residue and carb-dense
  • Skip raw veg add-ins tonight; focus on noodles, protein, and broth
  • Massage will flush your legs — drink water before bed
  • This is your highest carb day; eat steadily throughout the day, not one giant dinner
Wed May 2130 min jog AMgut-friendly
~550g carbs
  • Short jog just to shake out legs; don't treat it as a workout
  • Fibre restriction now firm: white rice, white bread, white pasta only at dinner
  • Avoid anything new or unusual — not the night to try a new restaurant
May 22–23 — taper & gut protection
Carbs stay high but fat and fibre come way down. Goal is a full gut on glycogen, empty gut on race morning.
Thu May 22restlow fibre · low fat
~500–550g carbs
  • Eat familiar, simple foods all day — nothing adventurous
  • Dinner: white pasta or white rice with a lean protein (chicken, fish); light sauce
  • Avoid: red meat, fried food, creamy sauces, anything spicy, raw salads, legumes
  • Finish dinner by 7–8pm; don't go to bed stuffed
Fri May 2315 min jog AMvery low fibre
~500g carbs
  • Jog is just to calm nerves and keep legs feeling alive — genuinely short and easy
  • Breakfast: white toast, banana, eggs if you want protein — nothing heavy
  • Lunch: familiar carbs — white rice bowl, plain pasta, bagel
  • Pre-race dinner (eat 7–8pm): white pasta or rice, lean protein, olive oil or light sauce only — no heavy fat
  • Snack before bed if hungry: banana, white toast with honey, or rice cakes
  • Hydrate well all day; include some sodium (salty crackers, pretzels) — not just water
  • Lay out all your gear and nutrition tonight
May 24 — race day
Race morning
4:30 AMWake up. Drink a glass of water immediately.
4:45 AMBathroom time — give your gut every chance to clear before you leave.
5:00 AMBreakfast: 2–3 pieces white toast with honey or jam + banana + sports drink or juice. Target ~100–120g carbs. Keep fat and protein low — no eggs, no peanut butter.
5:00–6:30Sip water or diluted Skratch. Don't guzzle — stay comfortable.
6:30 AMOptional: half a banana or a few rice cakes if stomach feels empty. Stop eating 30 min before gun.
7:00 AMGun goes. First XACT bar or gummies at 30 min, then alternate every 30 min as practiced.
